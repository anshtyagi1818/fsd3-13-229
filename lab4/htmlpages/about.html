Heritage & Design

Sculpted by Wind and Time.

Our design ethos, "Fluidic Aerodynamics," combines organic natural curves with razor-sharp aerodynamic precision to minimize drag coefficient while maximizing aesthetic dominance.

Wind-Tunnel Perfection

Every body panel, air intake, and rear diffuser is tested for over 500 hours in cryogenic wind tunnels to achieve an ultra-low 0.19 Cd drag coefficient.

Handcrafted Interiors

Sourced from sustainably managed alpine tanneries and cruelty-free vegan alternatives, our cabin materials balance tactile warmth with cutting-edge holographic displays.

Aura Matrix Lighting

Dynamic micro-LED optical clusters communicate intention with pedestrians and adapt beam throws in real time using neural AI pathway prediction.

Milestones

Our Automotive Journey.

From a boutique design studio to a global leader in electric luxury.

2018

Foundation in Munich

Creta Automotive is established by a collective of veteran Porsche and Tesla engineers with a vision to build the ultimate driver's electric vehicle.

2021

Unveiling the Creta Alpha Concept

Debut of our revolutionary solid-state battery architecture and fluidic aerodynamics concept at the Geneva Motor Show, winning Best Design of the Year.

2024

Zero-Carbon Gigafactory Opens

Our flagship production facility in Stuttgart goes live, operating entirely on localized wind and solar generation with zero net carbon footprint.

2026 & Beyond

Global Expansion & Apex GT-e

Launching deliveries across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific, setting new benchmarks for autonomous capability and track performance.
